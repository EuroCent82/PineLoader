Developer note - Unofficial community software

PineLoader is an independent community project. It is not reviewed or supported by the game studio. Use community software at your own risk and back up saves before installing mods.

PineLoader 1.1.0-alpha is a prerelease for testing. It starts Pionero Capital before loading compatible mods. It does not include mods.
